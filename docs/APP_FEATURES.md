# Tatum Tech App Features

This document provides a comprehensive overview of the Tatum Tech App features, written in clear, high-level language for non-technical readers including marketing, design, and development teams.

## 🏠 Home

Your personal dashboard. From here, explore all areas of Tatum Tech events, coding challenges, partners, and more. The home screen features a horizontal pager with five main categories: Events, Coding, Community, Career, and Games. Each category displays relevant sections in an organized grid layout, making it easy to navigate to different features of the app.

## 🎉 Events

Discover upcoming Tatum Tech events, register, and network with digital contact cards. The Events section includes:

- **Upcoming Events**: View events and RSVP via Luma; open Virtual Speakers sessions with Google Meet Join links
- **Digital Networking**: Create a Tatum Tech contact card, share it via QR code, and scan others' cards to save one-way connections (no friend requests or chat)
- **Scanner**: QR code scanner for contact-card networking and general event QR scans
- **Partners**: Learn about organizations that support Tatum Tech's mission
- **Resources**: Access event-related materials and learning resources

## 👾 Coding

Practice coding challenges in multiple languages, track your streaks, and strengthen your problem-solving skills. Designed for both beginners and future professionals. The Coding section includes:

- **Coding Challenges**: Interactive programming challenges in various languages (Kotlin, JavaScript, Python, C#, C++)
- **Stats**: Track your progress, streaks, and achievements
- **Resources**: Access coding tutorials, documentation, and learning materials

## 🧑‍🤝‍🧑 Community

Connect with peers, mentors, and partners through Discord and community resources. Stay up-to-date with announcements and collaborative opportunities. The Community section includes:

- **Community**: Join the Tatum Tech Discord server and connect with other learners
- **Donate**: Support the mission with financial contributions
- **Resources**: Access community-driven learning materials and collaboration tools

## 💼 Career

Browse real job openings from our partners in the video-game and tech industry. Apply directly or visit our community Discord for the latest opportunities. The Career section includes:

- **Apply for Jobs**: View current job openings from partner companies like Riot Games, Microsoft, Google, and Sony
- **Resources**: Access career development materials, resume tips, and interview preparation guides

## ❤️ Donate

Support the mission. Every donation helps us provide more computers and learning tools to youth across South LA. The donation system allows users to contribute financially to Tatum Tech's mission of bridging the digital divide.

## 🌟 Stats

Track your journey — see how many events you've attended, challenges you've completed, and streaks you've maintained. The Stats section provides comprehensive analytics including:

- Events attended
- Coding challenges completed
- QR codes scanned
- Achievement progress (Discover Games views, Career Apply clicks, coding, networking, and more)
- Current learning streaks
- Overall progress metrics

From Stats, **View All** opens the full Achievements list without crashing; badge icons are loaded from string drawable names in `achievements.json`.

## 🎮 Games

Discover and explore games from our partners and developers — complete with media previews, descriptions, and store links. The Games section mimics a modern app store experience, highlighting partner content and providing easy access to game downloads.

### Game Categories

- **Featured**: Swipeable carousel of highlighted games (image, name, short description, CTA)
- **Just Too Fun**: Enterprise subscription games
- **Tatum Games Favorites**: Startup subscription games
- **Apps in Development**: Games that are not yet released
- **Casual Gamer**: Free subscription casual games
- **Core Gamer**: Free subscription non-casual games

The Discover Games area uses two primary tabs — **Featured** and **Games** — instead of older discovery rows such as Top 10, Action, Strategy, Trending, or Sponsored.

### Game Features

Each game includes comprehensive information:
- **Game Details**: Title, developer, category, and release status
- **Media Gallery**: Screenshots, promotional videos, and gameplay trailers
- **Descriptions**: Short previews and detailed game descriptions
- **Download Links**: Direct links to Android, iOS, Steam, and official websites
- **Subscription Info**: Clear indication of subscription requirements and access levels

### User Experience

- **Featured Carousel**: Large, eye-catching banners for highlighted games
- **Grid Layout**: Organized game cards with thumbnails and key information
- **Search & Filter**: Easy discovery of games by category, developer, or subscription type
- **Offline Support**: Games catalog works without internet connection
- **Partner Integration**: Seamless integration with partner game stores and platforms

## 🏢 Partners

See the organizations that make Tatum Tech possible. Explore their missions, learn about their work, and visit their websites. The Partners section showcases:

- **Riot Games**: Creators of League of Legends and strong supporters of South LA tech education
- **Microsoft**: Technology giant supporting diversity in tech through education and mentorship programs
- **Google**: Leading tech company committed to increasing representation in the tech industry
- **Sony Interactive Entertainment**: Gaming industry leader supporting youth development in technology and gaming
- **Epic Games**: Creators of Fortnite and Unreal Engine, supporting game development education

## 📚 Resources

Access recommended books, websites, and learning materials to help you build your skills beyond our events. Coding → Resources includes filter chips (All, Java, Kotlin, C++, C#, Python, AI/ML, JavaScript, PHP) matching Career’s chip pattern, plus a friendly empty state when a category has no vetted links yet. The catalog prioritizes official documentation and reputable educational sources.

## 🔔 Notifications

Stay informed about the latest updates, achievements, and opportunities. The notification system provides:

- **Coding Challenge Updates**: Notifications about new challenges and achievements
- **Event Reminders**: Registration confirmations and event updates
- **Achievement Badges**: Celebrations for completing milestones
- **Community Updates**: Latest discussions and announcements
- **Career Opportunities**: New job postings from partner companies

## 👤 Profile & Demographic Info

From the app drawer, users can manage their account details and optionally share demographic information:

- **Profile**: Update username, name, and email
- **Demographic Info**: Optionally provide age range, sex, occupation, salary range, and school/district information used for aggregate event analytics and reporting (with required age and data-usage consent)
